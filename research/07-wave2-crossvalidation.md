# Wave 2: Adversarial Cross-Validation of Load-Bearing Claims (2026-07-18)

| Claim | Net verdict | Design impact |
|---|---|---|
| GEPA best for agents | Holds single-agent (MLflow/Databricks ship `GepaPromptOptimizer` natively). Weakened for multi-agent coordination (MAS-PromptBench, arXiv:2606.23664) and **continual settings: GEPA-optimized agents can transfer BELOW unoptimized baseline on new tasks unless regression control is built in** (arXiv:2607.14004) | Our frozen-holdout + baseline-diff discipline is not optional — it's what makes optimization gains durable. Optimize per-component (single-agent scope), not whole-system topology |
| PoLL panel-of-small-judges beats single big judge | **CONTRADICTED by newer, larger studies**: "Nine Judges, Two Effective Votes" (arXiv:2605.29800) — correlated errors mean 9 judges ≈ 2 independent votes; single best judge matched/exceeded full panel in all conditions. RoPoLL: panels retain value only for robustness under adversarial/corrupted inputs | **Default = single strong judge (different model family than the app), calibrated against human labels.** Panels only as an optional robustness mode. Binary>Likert and one-call-per-dimension still hold (pairwise verdicts flip ~35% on rewording vs 9% for binary — orq.ai) |
| pass^k reliability metric | Holds as necessary floor; 2026 work extends it (Reliability Decay Curve, Meltdown Onset — arXiv:2603.29231; frontier models had HIGHEST meltdown rates, up to 19%) | Keep pass^k; optionally add per-horizon decay reporting later |
| Tool descriptions ≥ prompts | **Holds strongly, better evidence than Anthropic's blog**: MCP description-smell study (10,831 servers): smells cost 8.8–11.6pp selection accuracy; compliant descriptions 72% vs 20% baseline (arXiv:2602.18914). Rewriting descriptions ALONE: +60.89% query success (arXiv:2602.20426) | Tool descriptions are a first-class optimization surface — possibly the highest-leverage one |
| Dataset sizing 20-50/100+/few-hundred | Holds directionally; OpenAI/Google/Microsoft/Databricks docs deliberately give NO numbers (Databricks caps datasets at 2,000 rows, favors production-trace sourcing) | Keep the heuristic, present as practitioner guidance not vendor gospel |
| Small-n statistics (was a GAP — now filled) | Concrete guidance exists: paired-difference analysis on identical question sets; **CLT-based CIs fail below n≈100 → Bayesian methods** (Cameron Wolfe, cameronrwolfe.substack.com/p/stats-llm-evals); McNemar's-test framework for paired per-sample comparison, sensitive to 0.3pp (arXiv:2602.10144, in LM Eval Harness); sequential/Thompson sampling (NeurIPS 2025); tooling: evalstats (github.com/ianarawjo/evalstats) | Bake into run/optimize skills: paired comparisons on identical cases, McNemar or paired bootstrap for keep/revert decisions, Bayesian CIs under n≈100. A "better" score without significance = no decision |
| Synthetic ≈ real? | Holds strongly as a RISK: "Coverage Illusion" — synthetic queries said 90% needed augmentation, real traffic 27.8% (arXiv:2605.27220); Airbnb needed real-seed blending to cut divergence 7.5× (arXiv:2605.21812); **generator-discriminator collapse: synthetic evals from a sibling model of the system under test inflate scores while real users drift away undetected** | Generator diversity matters (vary personas/styles/models); seed from real examples ASAP; periodic real-trace grounding is mandatory, not optional; note our generator (Claude) may share family with target app's model — mitigate with real seeds + human review of datasets |

## Cumulative design changes from wave 2 so far
1. Trajectory scoring: own scripts + agentevals/DeepEval (promptfoo trajectory assertions refuted — see 06).
2. Judge default: single strong calibrated judge, not panel.
3. Statistics: paired tests (McNemar/paired bootstrap), Bayesian CIs for small n — in the keep/revert gate.
4. Deterministic replay with recorded tool responses: build ourselves (no vendor has it).
5. Synthetic data: real-seed blending + diversity + human dataset review as guardrails against generator-collapse.
6. Optimization durability requires our baseline/holdout regime (GEPA gains don't transfer without it).
